# Timeline Estimate

## What's being estimated

Rollout timeline for BrainyFuture to adopt the Use Case Readiness diagnostic as a standard first step
across all ten consultants, using Jane's fintech case as the pilot example.

## Phase 1: Pilot, single consultant, single client (Weeks 1–2)

One consultant uses the diagnostic on real incoming client cases, starting with a case like Jane's.
Goal: confirm the diagnostic's output is accurate enough and useful enough to trust in a live client
conversation, not just a demo. Cost: minimal, the module already exists and is live.

## Phase 2: Rollout to all ten consultants (Weeks 3–5)

Once the pilot consultant confirms the tool holds up in real conversations, roll out access to the
remaining nine. Includes a short internal session, roughly half a day, walking consultants through how
to read a diagnostic report, when to trust it, and when a case needs deeper manual review beyond what
the tool flags.

## Phase 3: Standard practice (Week 6 onward)

Diagnosis-before-recommendation becomes a standard step for every new client engagement, not optional.
Chleo gets a shared, standardized record across all ten consultants instead of ten private judgment
calls.

## Assumptions, stated plainly

- Timeline assumes the diagnostic module continues running on its current live infrastructure, no major
  rebuild required for this rollout.
- Assumes minimal resistance to adoption; a consultant unconvinced after the pilot phase would extend
  Phase 2 while the tool's outputs are refined.
- This is a Round 1, pitch-level timeline. Round 2's strategic deployment plan will define pilot success
  criteria explicitly and extend this into a full POC-to-pilot-to-deployment plan.
