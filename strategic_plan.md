# Strategic Deployment and Commercialisation Plan

## Phases

**Phase 1: POC**
Current state. Groundwork diagnostic confirms the use case is safe to build. n8n flow proves the AI draft can call the diagnostic and return a verdict. No real customer data touched yet.

**Phase 2: Pilot**
AI draft system runs on a subset of real complaints, one support team, limited volume. Human review stays mandatory. Compliance docs finalized for this scope, not just planned.

**Phase 3: Full Deployment**
Rolled out to all support agents handling loan complaints. Monitoring and logging fully active. Re-diagnosis process in place for any scope change.

**Optional Phase 4: Scale**
Extend to other complaint types beyond payment delays, or to other BrainyFuture clients in lending. Each extension re-enters Use Case Readiness before build.

## Timeline and Milestones

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| POC | Complete | Diagnostic verdict returned, n8n flow tested end to end |
| Pilot | 6-8 weeks | AI draft live for one team, review workflow tested with real complaints |
| Full Deployment | 8-10 weeks after pilot greenlight | All support agents onboarded, monitoring live |
| Scale (optional) | Ongoing | Case by case, each new use case re-diagnosed first |

## Go-to-Market

- Buyers: mid-size EU fintech and lending companies with manual complaint handling and no in-house AI governance process.
- Channel: direct through BrainyFuture's consulting relationships, Groundwork as the diagnostic entry point for every new client conversation.
- Pricing: diagnostic-first engagement model. Groundwork readiness check priced separately from build work, so clients pay for the risk assessment even if they decide not to build.
- Differentiator: most AI consultancies sell builds first and governance later, if at all. BrainyFuture leads with a documented risk verdict before recommending anything, and grounds every flagged risk in real incident evidence, not general warnings.

## Stakeholder Communication Plan

- Jane and her leadership team: monthly written status during pilot, covering response time data, review override rate, any compliance flags raised.
- Support agents: weekly check-in during pilot only, feedback folded into the review workflow design.
- BrainyFuture consultants: Groundwork verdict and evidence shared before any recommendation goes to a client, so every consultant works from the same standard.

## KPIs per Phase

**Pilot:**
- Average response time, before and after
- Percentage of AI drafts sent unedited by human reviewer
- Number of compliance flags raised, target zero unresolved by end of pilot

**Full Deployment:**
- Response time reduction sustained across full team
- Customer complaint resolution satisfaction, if survey data available
- Zero replies sent without human review, tracked continuously

**Greenlight condition, pilot to full deployment:**
Response time improves as targeted, human override rate stays low enough to show the AI drafts are useful not just present, and zero unresolved compliance flags at pilot close.

## Commercialisation Model

Diagnostic-as-a-service for the readiness check, paired with implementation consulting for any build BrainyFuture recommends. Groundwork itself becomes a standing internal tool that also demonstrates BrainyFuture's judgment to prospective clients, not a one-off deliverable.
